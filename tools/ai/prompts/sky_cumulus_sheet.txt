A 2x2 sprite sheet of four different big fluffy cumulus clouds for a cozy 3D game sky, Studio Ghibli inspired hand-painted style: bright warm-white sunlit tops with soft painterly edges, gentle lavender-blue and pale grey-blue shading on the undersides and in the folds, rounded billowing shapes stacked in layers, slightly flat bottoms. Each cloud sits centred in its own quarter of the image with a clear empty margin around it so the quarters can be cut apart; no cloud touches the edges or another quarter. Wide clouds (wider than tall). Transparent background, no sky colour behind the clouds, no text, no birds, no ground.

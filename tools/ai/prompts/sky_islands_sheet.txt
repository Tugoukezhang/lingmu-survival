A 2x2 sprite sheet of four different small distant floating sky islands for the background of a cozy 3D game, Studio Ghibli inspired painterly style, seen from the side at a distance: a grassy top with a few round trees (one has a tiny cottage, one a little windmill), rocky cliffs underneath tapering to a point with hanging roots, two of them with a thin waterfall pouring off the edge and fading into mist below. Soft atmospheric perspective: slightly hazy, pale blue tint in the shadows. Each island centred in its own quarter with an empty margin around it so the quarters can be cut apart. Transparent background, no sky, no clouds around them except the waterfall mist, no text.
